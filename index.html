Fun fact about our formulation:

1/3 of our formula is made of upcycled lemons. We harness the natural antibacterial and odor-neutralizing power of lemons, giving new life to what would otherwise be discarded.

What makes Lihmon different from other natural deodorants?

Unlike many natural deodorants that struggle with effectiveness, Lihmon took two years of rigorous testing to perfect. Our formula blends the power of lemon water with carefully chosen organic ingredients to neutralize odor, keep you feeling fresh, and nourish your skin—all without harsh chemicals or baking soda.

Will Lihmon work for an active lifestyle?

Absolutely! Lihmon was designed for people who move—whether you’re hitting the gym, flowing through yoga, or running errands. It’s sweat-tested and performance-proven to keep up with your most active days.

Is Lihmon safe for sensitive skin?

Yes! We formulated Lihmon with gentle, skin-loving ingredients. Unlike many deodorants that use baking soda (which can cause irritation), our lemon-powered formula is pH-balanced and free from harsh additives, making it perfect for sensitive skin.
